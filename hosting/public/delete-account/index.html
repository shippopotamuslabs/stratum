Delete your Stratum account

For the Stratum Android app (developer listing name: Stratum / package com.aether.stratum).

Self-serve deletion You can delete your account and associated data from inside the app. Use the steps below. We also process reasonable email requests within a commercially reasonable time (typically within 30 days) if you cannot use the in-app control.

Delete from the app (recommended)

  1. Open Stratum on your Android device and sign in with Google.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. This permanently removes your Stratum account and training data from this device and the cloud. Your Google Account is not deleted.
  5. After deletion you are signed out and returned to the sign-in screen. You may also revoke Stratum’s access under your Google Account permissions if it still appears there.

Email request (if you cannot use the app)

  1. Email browntown.development@gmail.com from the same Google account email you use to sign in to Stratum.
  2. Use a subject line like Stratum account deletion request.
  3. In the body, state that you want your Stratum account and associated data deleted, and include the Google account email used in the app.

What we delete

When account deletion completes, we remove from our systems:

  • Your Firebase Auth account for Stratum (where applicable)
  • Cloud training data tied to that account (for example programs, workouts, logs, preferences, import job metadata, and related sync documents we store for the app)
  • Local training data on the device when you delete from the app
  • Associated support emails you sent about a manual request, after we’re done

What we may keep (limited retention)

  • Short-lived backups or logs needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance, deleted or rotated on normal retention schedules
  • Aggregated or de-identified analytics that cannot reasonably identify you
  • Records we are required to keep by law (if any apply to a specific request)

What deletion does not do

  • It does not delete your Google Account itself—only Stratum’s account and our app data for that sign-in.
  • Log out (without deleting) only clears local data on that device; cloud data remains so it can return when you sign back in.
  • Uninstalling the app alone does not delete cloud data; use Delete account (or email us) for a full wipe.
