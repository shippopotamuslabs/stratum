Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Stratum (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use the Stratum Android app and related backend services. It is meant to be clear and short. Contact us if something is unclear.

1. Who we are

Stratum is a workout log and programming companion. For privacy questions or data requests, email browntown.development@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Stratum, we may process:

  • Account information — identifiers from Google Sign-In (for example user ID, display name, and email associated with the Google account you choose).
  • Training data you provide — programs, workouts, lifts, sets, weights, notes, gym or equipment preferences, onboarding answers, imports, and similar content you enter or upload.
  • App and device signals — technical data needed to run and secure the service (for example app version, crash diagnostics, and App Check / integrity signals).
  • Support messages — whatever you send us by email when you contact support.

We do not require you to submit special medical diagnoses. Training logs can still reflect health-related activity (for example sessions completed). Treat that data carefully.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • Store and sync your training data across devices when cloud sync is enabled.
  • Power product features such as program generation, progression helpers, imports, and optional cloud-assisted suggestions.
  • Operate, secure, debug, and improve the app (including crash reporting).
  • Respond to your requests and enforce our Terms of Use.

4. Cloud AI / language model processing

Some features may send limited, feature-related prompts or structured context to a cloud language model through our own backend (Firebase Callable Functions). Provider API keys are not shipped in the app. Content you include in those features may be processed by that backend and the model provider solely to fulfill the request. Do not put secrets or sensitive personal data into prompts or imports that you would not want processed for that purpose.

5. Third parties and processors

We use infrastructure providers to run Stratum, including:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Storage (where used), App Check, and Crashlytics.
  • Google Play — distribution, updates, and (when offered) billing for subscriptions or in-app products.
  • Model inference providers — only via our backend when a cloud-assisted feature is used (not as a direct client integration from the app).

These parties process data under their own terms and privacy policies when they act as independent controllers, or under our instructions when they act as processors.

6. Retention

We keep account and training data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Local data on a device is typically removed when you sign out on that device or uninstall the app. Cloud data is retained until you delete your account (or we delete it in response to a valid request), subject to reasonable backups, legal holds, and security logs.

7. Account deletion and your choices

Sign out clears local training data on that device; cloud data generally remains associated with your account so it can return when you sign back in.

Delete account: We intend to ship an in-app “Delete account” control that removes your account and associated cloud training data from our systems (subject to short technical delays, backups, and any data we must keep for legal or security reasons). Until that control is available—or if it fails—email browntown.development@gmail.com from the address on your account and request deletion. We will process reasonable requests within a commercially reasonable time.

You can also stop using Stratum at any time and revoke the app’s Google access from your Google account settings.

8. Children

Stratum is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard platform controls (including Firebase Authentication and App Check where configured). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; use the app with that understanding.

10. International processing

We may process information in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. Those locations may have different data-protection rules than your home country.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” will change when we do. Material changes may also be called out in the app or store listing when practical. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests: browntown.development@gmail.com

See also our Terms of Use.
