# Stratum v1.71.0

Minor bump under `contracts/RELEASE.md` §1.2: the range since
v1.70.0 replaces the equipment delete confirmation with a tall
sheet and a full-screen replacement picker (STR-335), a change
a user sees and uses. The versionCode is derived from the
first-parent commit count of the build checkout, as since
STR-205.

## What changed

- Equipment delete sheet (STR-335, PR #163). Deleting a piece
  of equipment from Manage Equipment or Equipment Increments
  opens a tall bottom sheet. The sheet lists every linked lift
  and workout as its own card in a scrolling region, with the
  actions pinned below the list, so the full list stays
  reachable on any screen size. Choosing a replacement opens a
  full-screen catalog picker with search; the delete action
  stays disabled until a replacement is picked. Equipment with
  no linked lifts completes through the bodyweight fallback in
  the sheet itself.
